SPEC-357b — CONSOLE LOG ENCODING, AND HOW TO VERIFY THE DIGEST
==============================================================

The two 28,800 s diagnosis cells (spec357-diag-r1, spec357-diag-r2) each produced a
harness console log and a server console log of ~86-88 MB. Every prior cell in this
lineage committed console logs of 32-64 KB. The ~1,400x difference is NOT a change in
what the cells did: it is the WAL-watermark alarm storm that SPEC-357a's own
SOAK_SERVER_LOG_PASSTHROUGH made visible for the first time (99 % of the bytes; see the
POST-DATA finding record published in spec356-manifest.md §11.1+).

DECISION (user ruling, 2026-08-14): committing 349 MB of near-identical repeated lines
into a public repository's permanent history was judged not worth its cost, so the four
full logs are committed GZIPPED and a plain-text extract of the load-bearing lines is
committed beside them.

WHAT IS COMMITTED
-----------------
  spec357-diag-r{1,2}.harness-console.log.gz          full, lossless, ~1.8 MB each
  spec357-diag-r{1,2}.server-console.log.gz           full, lossless, ~1.8 MB each
  spec357-diag-r{1,2}.server-console.residency.log    plain text, ANSI-stripped, the
                                                      1,796 / 1,642 residency lines the
                                                      per-epoch ledgers are built from

The .artifacts.sha256 files are the RUNNER'S OWN output and are committed UNMODIFIED.
They name the UNCOMPRESSED basenames, because that is what the runner digested. This is
deliberate: the digest is evidence and is not rewritten to match a packaging choice.

HOW TO VERIFY THE DIGEST
------------------------
Decompress in place and run the runner's own digest file:

    gunzip -k spec357-diag-r1.harness-console.log.gz
    shasum -a 256 -c spec357-diag-r1.artifacts.sha256

Both cells were verified this way at commit time: the digest line for
harness-console.log matches the decompressed bytes exactly, and server-console.log
decompresses to a stream identical to the original (server-console.log is not itself a
digest-covered artifact — the runner's ADJ-20 binding covers seven files).

WHY THE EXTRACT EXISTS ALONGSIDE
--------------------------------
The residency lines are the load-bearing content: spec357-residency.sh consumes them to
build the entry/exit ledgers. Keeping them in plain text means a reader can inspect the
actual measurement without decompressing 86 MB, and keeps the diff-visible surface of
this commit proportionate to the evidence it carries. The extract is derived, not
primary: it is reproducible from the .gz with

    gunzip -c spec357-diag-r1.server-console.log.gz \
      | sed 's/\x1b\[[0-9;]*m//g' \
      | grep 'topgun_server::tombstone_frontier::residency:'

NOTE ON THE ANSI CODES
----------------------
The raw capture carries ANSI SGR sequences, which split the tracing module target from
its trailing colon and are one half of why the pinned extractor matched zero lines on
the raw stream. The .gz files preserve the raw bytes including those sequences; the
extract has them stripped. See spec357b-console-normalize.sh and the POST-DATA finding
record for the full account.
