TWO SLOT READINGS THAT ARE RAW OBSERVATIONS, PUBLISHED HERE WITHOUT INTERPRETATION. They are recorded

      `if (dC > 0)` -- it does not divide by a zero denominator. Measured directly from the same
      ledgers: over the coordinate last half [14,410 ... 28,800] s,
      `topgun_or_prune_considered_total` is 35,000 at both endpoints on r1 and 99,000 at both
      endpoints on r2, so `dC = 0` on both, while `topgun_or_prune_passes_total` advances by
      440,069 (r1) and 358,370 (r2) over the same window. The slot's absence is the driver declining
      an undefined quotient, not a truncated run.

  (2) `B` IS `UNDEFINED (100 % sentinel, 0 non-sentinel rows)` and
      `MEDIAN_L_OVER_B` is withheld, on BOTH cells, with `B_SENTINEL_EXCLUDED_PCT` = 100.000000.
