FINDING 1 — P8 could not fail under the mutation it exists to refute (fixed before the arm)
===========================================================================================
On the FIRST application of mutation (ii), P8 (`arrival_order_changes_nothing`) stayed GREEN.

Cause: `registration_trace` registered the whole set in silence and only then queried the
proposal. A ceiling that clamps itself against a previously published value therefore never had
a published value to clamp against, and the two arrival orders produced identical traces. The
property was un-falsifiable by the first-claimant latch — the exact shape Constraint 12 forbids
("a witness that cannot fail is not a witness").

Fix, committed as ccb6d87e BEFORE the recorded arm: `registration_trace` now queries the
proposal after every registration, outside the recorded trace, so the latch leaves the arrival
order visible in the FINAL state the trace compares. The observations themselves are unchanged,
so the property stays TRUE for the correct implementation (verified: full lib suite green at
ccb6d87e).

Both the pre-fix (green) and post-fix (red) mutation-(ii) runs are recorded below.
