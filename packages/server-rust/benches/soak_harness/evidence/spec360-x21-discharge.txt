================================================================================
THE X21 DISCHARGE — three limbs, each bound to the transport that actually
carries its terms
================================================================================

Standing rule X21 asks, of every assertion this increment makes, WHICH transport
carries the term being asserted. This file discharges it limb by limb, records
the executed evidence for the load-bearing limb, and states the join key.

The three limbs are disjoint by transport, not by subject matter: the same
epoch's story is told across all three, and the join between them is stated in
§4 rather than left implicit.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THE ORDERING PREMISE EVERY METRICS ASSERTION HERE RESTS ON
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Prometheus recorder is bound FIRST and the frontier is constructed INSIDE
that binding — `metrics::with_local_recorder(&recorder, || { let f =
one_epoch_frontier(...); ... })`.

This is not a stylistic preference. `MetricsPruneRecorder` resolves EVERY metric
handle ONCE, at construction, and touches each series with `increment(0)` so it
renders from the first scrape. Two consequences follow, and both are load-bearing
below:

  (a) INVERT the order and every handle binds to a no-op for its whole lifetime.
      The series then never appears in the render, and a reader that treated an
      absent series as a zero would pass VACUOUSLY. This is why the reader used
      here panics on an absent series (see §3) instead of defaulting.

  (b) BECAUSE the handles resolve at construction, an increment issued from a
      SPAWNED task still lands on the recorder that was bound when the frontier
      was built. The recorder-binding gap that makes `tracing`'s thread-local
      `set_default` unreadable across a spawn DOES NOT APPLY to metrics. This is
      the single fact that makes limb (c) readable at all, and it is also the
      fact the `W6` fallback arm would have rested on had it been selected.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. LIMB (a) — RECORD TRANSPORT: RENDERED-ROW RECONSTRUCTION, STATED AS SUCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STATUS: discharged as a RENDERED-TRANSPORT limb. It is NOT a struct-transport
limb, and the earlier premise that it was is withdrawn on the record.

WHY THE STRUCT PREMISE IS FALSE AT HEAD, in the tree's own terms:

  - `prune_epoch_tombstones` returns `()`. Its `PrunePassRecord` local is built,
    handed to the observer, and dropped. It is never yielded to a caller.
  - `drain_prunable_tombstones` returns `Vec<(Epoch, TombstoneRef)>` and consumes
    its `PruneEpochResidencyRecord`s INTERNALLY via `publish_epoch_exit`.
  - The only observer seam is `prune_observer`, a `Box<dyn PruneRecordObserver>`
    selected from an environment variable inside `TombstoneFrontier::new`. There
    is NO injection constructor and no setter.

SURFACE IS NOT TRANSPORT. The obvious way to make the two records reachable
in-process would be to add an injection constructor or a capturing-observer seam
to `TombstoneFrontier`. That would be a production API surface whose only
consumer is a test, and it is forbidden: no injection constructor, no
capturing-observer seam, and no test-only `pub`/`pub(crate)` surface is added to
`TombstoneFrontier` by this increment. `git diff` on `tombstone_frontier.rs` and
`tombstone_frontier_impl.rs` shows no new constructor or observer-setter
signature.

HOW THE RECORDS ARE OBTAINED INSTEAD: both are RECONSTRUCTED, field by field,
from the rendered `tracing` rows the production code already emits — the exit row
and the pass row — captured in-test by a thread-local capture layer. The
reconstructed values are then fed to the classifier. The in-tree precedent for
exactly this reconstruction over a service-path drive already existed before this
increment and is followed rather than invented.

ITS EPISTEMIC STATUS CHANGES WITH THE RE-SITING, and this is stated rather than
glossed: the classifier is fed values that came off a WIRE FORMAT, so the
correctness of the reconstruction is itself part of what is being asserted. That
is precisely why limb (b) — the rendered-text limb — is the load-bearing one and
why the extractor contract was frozen BEFORE anything rendered.

THE LIMB'S SCOPE IS BOUNDED BY WHAT THE ROWS ACTUALLY RENDER, and one term falls
outside it: the pass row carries `kind`, `considered` and `empty_drain` and
NOTHING ELSE. `epochs_drained` is sent only to the pass observer. It is therefore
NOT among the terms this limb reconstructs; it is re-attributed to limb (c).
Reading it off a row would PANIC in the reader ("field is absent from the
rendered row"), and widening the row to carry it would be a production emission
change whose only consumer is one test — forbidden.

2. LIMB (b) — LINE TRANSPORT. THE LOAD-BEARING LIMB.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STATUS: discharged, with executed evidence.

SUBJECT: assertions written against the RENDERED `tracing` text of
  (i)   the NEW removal line,
  (ii)  the pass row,
  (iii) the settlement row.

WHY THIS LIMB IS THE LOAD-BEARING ONE. The failure class this increment exists to
close is an emitter whose wire format defeats its own extractor: a downstream
reader keyed on a form the emitter never produced, scoring zero matches against
thousands of rows and reporting the zero as a finding. That class is closed AT
SOURCE here — by freezing the extractor contract before the emitter exists and
then asserting on rendered text — never by a downstream adapter.

THE EXTRACTOR CONTRACT (frozen before this increment rendered anything; the
frozen layer of the ruling artifact is the authority, this is a restatement for
the reader of this file):

  TARGET      topgun_server::tombstone_frontier::removal
              — its OWN target, distinct from the residency target and from the
                settlement target, which is what makes the line selectable
                WITHOUT a `kind` discriminant field. No `kind` field is added;
                the target does that work, and the field set stays exactly the
                eight it was adopted with.

  FIELDS      ts, op_seq, epoch, refs_returned, refs_at_entry, bytes_returned,
              watermark, ceiling — by NAME, in the order the emitting macro
              declares them. The COUNT is not asserted; the NAMES are.

  TYPES       op_seq, epoch, refs_returned, refs_at_entry, bytes_returned,
              watermark, ceiling are all unsigned integers rendered as bare
              decimal digits. `ts` in its explicit rendering is a signed Unix-ms
              integer, following the tree's existing convention. NO field is a
              float.

  SEPARATOR   one `name=value` pair per field, each followed by exactly ONE
              ASCII space. The row is prefixed with its target as a pair of the
              same shape. The two in-tree visitors differ by a leading space
              before `target=`, and which form a reader consumes is decided by
              that reader's own visitor.

  NO JSON     there is no JSON blob anywhere on this line. Every field is an
              individual `tracing` field. No consumer should attempt to parse a
              serialized structure out of it.

  ts          BOTH renderings were pre-registered as compliant: (a) the
              subscriber's own stamp, with `ts` not a recorded field at all; and
              (b) an explicit signed Unix-ms field. WHICH SHIPPED: (b), the
              EXPLICIT field. Recorded in the ruling artifact's post-section.

EXECUTED EVIDENCE — the rendered rows, verbatim from the drive's own transcript.
Note the single TRAILING space on each: the visitor writes `name=value ` per pair
and never trims. `ts` varies per run; every other term is fixed by the fixture.

  ORIGIN SIGNATURE (the planted present-but-empty entry, driven through the
  production prune service):

target=topgun_server::tombstone_frontier::removal message=prune removal observed ts=1787505799565 op_seq=2 epoch=1 refs_returned=0 refs_at_entry=1 bytes_returned=0 watermark=1 ceiling=2 

  POSITIVE CONTROL (the same fixture, unplanted — a genuinely non-empty removal):

target=topgun_server::tombstone_frontier::removal message=prune removal observed ts=1787505799565 op_seq=2 epoch=1 refs_returned=1 refs_at_entry=1 bytes_returned=4 watermark=1 ceiling=2 

  NEGATIVE CONTROL: on both paths that skip the removal arm — no eligible epoch
  (with a NON-zero watermark, so the silence is attributable to eligibility and
  not to the dark path) and the dark fast path itself — the capture contains
  ZERO rows on this target while remaining NON-EMPTY overall. Silence from this
  instrument is therefore MEANINGFUL rather than ambiguous.

WHAT MAKES THIS LIMB RED. The observation arm: emit `refs_at_entry` in place of
`refs.len()` for `refs_returned` — the minimal faithful encoding of the tautology
this whole lineage is trying to escape — and the rendered-text assertion FAILS,
naming `refs_returned != refs.len()`. Executed; transcript in the witness file.
That arm is what proves the line is an OBSERVATION and not a second copy of the
slot: `refs_returned` is read from the vector the index removal itself returned,
`refs_at_entry` is read from the slot, and the two are not the same expression.

Note which arm the mutation REDs on, because it is informative: the POSITIVE
control stays GREEN under it (there `refs.len()` and `refs_at_entry` are both 1,
so the swap is invisible), and only the ORIGIN-SIGNATURE arm discriminates. A
witness set containing only a positive control would have graded this mutation
green — which is the whole reason all three readings are exercised.

3. LIMB (c) — METRICS TRANSPORT. TWO DISJOINT SCOPES.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Conflating these two scopes is a known error class, so they are separated
explicitly.

3.1 — REASONED N/A FOR THE NEW LINE
-----------------------------------

STATUS: N/A, and the reason is a reason rather than an omission.

THE NEW INSTRUMENT HAS NO METRICS TRANSPORT. It was deliberately routed to the
LOG SINK and AWAY FROM THE METRICS REGISTRY. Asserting a metrics transport for it
would FABRICATE A CONSUMER THAT DOES NOT EXIST — the vacuous-assertion class in
reverse, and exactly the failure this discharge exists to prevent.

Concretely, and decidably:

  - NO `counter!`, `gauge!`, `histogram!` or `describe_*` is added for it. The
    pinned grep over `tombstone_frontier_impl.rs` returns 6 at the base and still
    returns 6 on the branch.
  - `PruneRecordObserver` gains NO method. No new observer-method signature
    appears in the branch diff.

This is a REASONED N/A. The limb is not silently skipped for this subject; it is
declared inapplicable, with the mechanism that makes it inapplicable named.

3.2 — LOAD-BEARING FOR `epochs_drained`
---------------------------------------

STATUS: discharged, and this is the ONLY place `epochs_drained` is read.

WHY IT LIVES HERE AND NOT IN LIMB (a). The rendered pass row does not carry
`epochs_drained`. It is sent only to the pass observer, and from there to a
Prometheus counter. The row is NOT widened to carry it — that would be a
production emission change whose only consumer is one test.

THE FULL RENDERED SERIES NAMES, given in full because the in-tree readers key on
the EXACT name and a short name is a RED rather than a soft miss:

    topgun_or_prune_considered_total
    topgun_or_prune_empty_drains_total
    topgun_or_prune_nonempty_drains_total
    topgun_or_prune_epochs_drained_total        <- the term this limb carries

THE READING RULE: the assertion must read `Some("...")` and NEVER `None`. The
in-tree reader returns an `Option` and the wrapper used by the drive PANICS on
`None`, naming the absent series. An absent series is a HARD ERROR and never a
zero — because the frontier touches every counter with `increment(0)` at
construction, so a series missing from a render means the handles were resolved
OUTSIDE the binding that render came from, which would make the whole comparison
vacuous.

WHY THE PROMETHEUS TRANSPORT IS READABLE FOR THIS WITNESS: because the handles
resolve at construction (§0(b)), an increment issued from a spawned task still
lands on the recorder bound when the frontier was built. The recorder-binding gap
that makes `tracing`'s `set_default` thread-local and unreadable across a spawn
DOES NOT APPLY to metrics. This is the same asymmetry an earlier increment in
this family already exploits to read a rendered counter off a spawned,
multi-threaded sweep and get a real number rather than an empty capture.

WHAT MAKES THIS LIMB RED — stated at exactly the strength the mechanism supports,
and no stronger:

  (i)  the asserted delta of 0 on `topgun_or_prune_epochs_drained_total` MOVES,
       which happens when the plant did not take (no eligible epoch was removed
       as planted) or when the drain RETURNED REFS; or
  (ii) the series renders ABSENT under an inverted recorder/frontier binding, so
       the reader returns `None` and the `Some("...")` reading rule fails.

  Neither failure mode is silent.

WHAT DOES *NOT* MAKE THIS LIMB RED, stated because the limb does not claim it:
the `:986` exit-attribution mutation is NOT a RED trigger here, and this limb
does not detect it. That mutation gates `drained_epochs.insert(e)`, and
`drained_epochs` feeds ONLY the exit attribution; the RETURNED `drained` vector
is built independently from the removed refs, and it is what the pass folds into
`epochs_drained`. Under the planted antecedent `drained` is empty WITH OR WITHOUT
that mutation, so this limb's delta stays 0 either way. That mutation's
discriminating assertion is the EXIT ROW, in limb (b), and that is where it REDs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE JOIN KEY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  PRIMARY KEY        `epoch`.
                     Populated on EVERY removal line, EVERY exit row and EVERY
                     settlement row. One epoch's story across all three limbs is
                     assembled on this and nothing else.

  SECONDARY ORDER    `op_seq`.
                     An ordering term, used to sequence rows that share an
                     epoch. It orders; it does not identify.

  `ts` IS NEVER A JOIN KEY.
                     Under EITHER of its two permitted renderings. It is a
                     DISPLAY field and is explicitly NON-JOINABLE. There is no
                     wall-clock key anywhere in this increment. Joining on a
                     wall clock across rows emitted by different components — or
                     worse, across hosts — manufactures correlations that the
                     data does not contain, and this family has already paid for
                     that lesson once.

5. WHAT IS AND IS NOT DISCHARGED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  DISCHARGED  limb (a) as a rendered-row reconstruction, with the struct premise
              withdrawn on the record and no seam added;
              limb (b) with executed rendered samples for all three readings the
              instrument can produce — silent, zero-returned, positive — plus the
              observation mutation arm;
              limb (c) as a reasoned N/A for the new line and as a load-bearing
              read for `epochs_drained` over the existing, unchanged counter.

  NOT CLAIMED This file records that the instrument EMITS CORRECTLY over the
              transport its consumer will read. It says NOTHING about what that
              instrument will observe under production or soak load. Reading it
              under load is a separate increment's job, and no reading of it is
              attempted, implied or pre-empted here.
